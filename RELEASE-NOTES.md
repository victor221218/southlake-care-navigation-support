# Care Navigation Support 2.13.0

- **View details** shows the information needed to compare services. Choose **Use this service** to get its contact details and follow-up question in chat.
- After the final pending service result, the chat offers **Keep chat open**, **Add to memory library**, and **Delete chat and notes**. Use **Case options → Finish conversation** to open the same choices for other chats.
- **Delete chat and notes** asks for confirmation before removing the conversation and its linked notes, ratings and outcomes.
- Close the previous app and open **Care-Navigation-Support-Setup-2.13.0.exe** to update.

Windows checks cover selection, in-chat feedback, the three completion choices, and deletion scoped to the conversation. Backup checks cover the links used to identify that feedback.
