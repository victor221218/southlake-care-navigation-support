# Care Navigation Support 2.12.0

- The **⋯** menu and **Case options** explain **Add to memory library** and **Delete chat history**. Save a useful conversation as one item or discard a practice or inaccurate chat.
- **Manage memory** groups provider notes, service results and related saved conversations by organization. Search by organization, service, or note. Expand a group to review and manage the full history.
- Ordinary chat turns stay together in the conversation. Say or type **Remember that …** to save one note. A selected card receives the note directly.
- Close the previous app and open **Care-Navigation-Support-Setup-2.12.0.exe** to update.

Windows checks cover organization search and expansion, saving or discarding a case, editing notes, and reopening the saved conversation.
