# Care Navigation Support 2.17.3

- Choose **Add a service** above chat to open the form immediately.
- Add-service requests open the form with **Category** at the top. The form can prefill a category or details you supplied.
- Save with any detail you have and complete the card later in **Known services**.

# Care Navigation Support 2.17.2

- Ask “Can you add a service for me?” to open the service form in chat.
- Fill in the details you have and choose **Save service**. Partial entries appear in Known services with **Needs details**.
- Choose **View saved service** in the conversation to return to the new card.

# Care Navigation Support 2.17.1

- Recommendation cards stay compact. Open **View details** and expand **Why this rank** for the card's explanation, or ask about the full order in chat.
- The inventory, tracking and chat outcome workflow from 2.17.0 is included.

# Care Navigation Support 2.17.0

- The inventory importer recognizes the **Service Inventory** column headings and lets you update matching cards while keeping their feedback.
- The **Navigation** worksheet imports reviewed provider outcomes into the local workspace. Known services shows recorded experience and missing source details.
- Report a clear service result by typing or speaking in chat. The app saves it locally. Ask for ranked service cards and then ask how the options were ranked.
- [The inventory and outcome guide](README.md#maintain-the-service-inventory-and-outcomes) describes the format, update steps and a fictional demo.

# Care Navigation Support 2.16.0

- **Known services** shows your entries first, followed by the public directory. Contacts show **(Own source)** or **(Public source)**.
- **Add a service** saves one entry. **Import services** reviews Excel .xlsx, CSV and TSV rows before saving them as service cards.
- The review asks about missing names, phone or email, and service area. Extra spreadsheet columns and source references remain available in **View details**.
- **Edit details** updates your own service and keeps its saved feedback. Notes and results carry their own source label in **View details** and **Manage memory**.
- The Windows installer and Mac test packages contain the same updated app. Mac launch testing and Apple notarization remain outstanding.
